01 What we do

The whole yard, handled.

Three services we run every season, and a fourth on the way. Book one or bundle them. Either way it's the same crew and the same standard.

  1. 01

    Mowing & Trimming

    Weekly or bi-weekly

    Our core service and the one we obsess over. Precise mowing, every edge trimmed clean, and a steady schedule, so the lawn looks freshly done all season and you never have to think about it.

  2. 02

    Seasonal Cleanups

    Spring + fall

    In spring we clear leaves, debris, and winter buildup to jump-start healthy growth. In fall we pull the leaves and get the lawn ready for winter. Either way, the property is left looking pristine.

  3. 03

    Landscape Detailing

    Hedges · Mulch · Small trees

    Precision hedge trimming, clean mulching, and careful small-tree trimming that brings back the shape and health of your beds and borders. The finishing work that makes a property look cared for.

  4. 04

    Snow Removal

    Coming soon

    Professional snow clearing and winter maintenance. Full details are on the way. Ask about it when you request a quote and we'll put you first in line.
